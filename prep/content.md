The next app I viewed was <a href="https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview">Scrivener</a>
  * Scrivener is paid, but allows users a 30 day free trial (only applicable for macOS and Window versions)
  * Provides a digital binder that stores manuscripts, world-building notes, character sketches, etc
  * Split-Screen views to allow 
  * Can stitch together separate scenes or chapters so you can read and edit them as if they were a standard document
