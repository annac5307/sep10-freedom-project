### Choosing a topic
I decided on the topic of fiction writing because I enjoy consuming such stories. I've always been entertained by how authors shape their worlds and the characters that live within it, as well as the way they evoke feelings through a simple diction choice or method of phrasing. In this world of ever-advancing technology, people, such as authors, should learn to utilize the tools provided online rather than dwelling in fear of replacement. I initially considered choosing graphic art, since I've always liked the process of creating graphics appealing to the eye. In fact, part of the reason I enjoy creating stories is due to the overlap in writing and graphics. Each description of shape, color, and behavior, helps convey the scenery's content and purpose in the narrative, similar to how every visual aspect in a graphic design is added with specific intentions towards the viewer.
