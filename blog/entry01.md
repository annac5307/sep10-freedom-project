# Entry 1: Topic decisions (Fiction Writing)
##### 10/19/26

### Choosing a topic
I decided on the topic of fiction writing because I enjoy consuming such stories. I've always been entertained by how authors shape their worlds and the characters that live within it, as well as the way they evoke feelings through a simple diction choice or method of phrasing. In this world of ever-advancing technology, people, such as authors, should learn to utilize the tools provided online rather than dwelling in fear of replacement. I initially considered choosing graphic art, since part of the reason I enjoy creating stories at all is due to the overlap in writing and graphics. Graphics, or the illusion of them through words, is what makes writing seem alive. I also considered picking the topic of the judiciary, but I found that my interest in fiction writing is stronger. 

The most useful of my chosen links are <a href= "https://j-nelson.net/2017/04/computer-programming-writing-fiction-iterative-processes/">Computer programming & writing fiction: Iterative processes | Jim Nelson</a> and <a href= "https://www.thenovelry.com/blog/book-writing-software">The Best Book Writing Software | The Novelry</a>. The first link reveals the aspects of writing and computer programming that inevitably converge, mostly in that every little change made within computer codes and stories can be a make or break to the viewers, showing points where the two fields can cooperate. The second link lists the pros and cons of various book-writing softwares, and this helps me further identify ways in which technology can assist writing.
